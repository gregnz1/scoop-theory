The method

A cup needs a point of view.

01

Anchor

The flavour you came for. Usually the richest, darkest, or most aromatic scoop in the cup.

02

Bridge

A shared note - nut, cocoa, fruit, caramel, or cream - makes the combination feel intentional.

03

Lift

Acid, fruit, bitterness, salt, or roast keeps cold sweetness from becoming flat and tiring.

ExceptionalExcellentStrongGoodAdventurous

Every pair and trio uses the same five-step verdict scale. The engine weighs classic flavour affinities, shared aromas, richness, acidity, bitterness, and texture. Verdicts are graded against the best cups the current cabinet can make, so Exceptional is kept for roughly the top tenth of our own suggestions, and Adventurous means less conventional than most possible cups. It treats a pairing as sensory judgement, not mathematical fact.

Source and safety

Flavour names are current. Pairing opinions are ours.

Cabinet names and rotation dates are checked against Gelato Messina's official monthly specials and classic flavour list. Store stock can differ and specials can sell out.

Do not use this guide for allergy decisions. Ingredients and cross-contact can change. Check the official allergen information and ask staff in store.
